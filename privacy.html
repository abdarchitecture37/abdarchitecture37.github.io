Privacy Policy – Told vs True Studio

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Told vs True Studio is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Told vs True YouTube channel to manage that channel. It uses YouTube API Services.

What data is accessed

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool reads and updates the owner's own YouTube channel data: videos, metadata, thumbnails, captions, playlists, comments and YouTube Analytics reports.

How it is used

Only to upload and manage videos on the Told vs True channel and to review its performance. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access credentials are kept in a private, access-controlled environment. Channel data is processed only as needed and is not kept longer than necessary.

Google and YouTube

By using this tool you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
